          Problem Set 5 · IKD

          My own agent: Interview Invite Catcher

          My agent card | Interview Invite Catcher | finished 20 October 2026, 8:45 AM SGT

          1. The job: Watches my Gmail inbox for interview invitations and alerts me straight away, so that I, a graduate student applying for jobs, never miss one or reply late.
          2. The trigger: A new email arrives in my Gmail with “interview” in the subject (Gmail search subject:interview -subject:"ACTION NEEDED").
          3. The tools: Gmail, New Email Matching Search (reads my inbox) and Gmail, Send Email (sends me one alert). Nothing else.
          4. It must never: Email anyone but me. The “To” field in the Send Email step is typed in as my own address, not mapped from the incoming email, so it cannot reply to a recruiter or anyone else.
          5. Where a person checks: I read each “ACTION NEEDED” alert when it arrives and open the original email before replying. Each evening I compare my inbox with Zap History to catch missed invites or false alarms.

          My prediction, written at 9:15 AM before running it. I will send myself an email titled “5 interview tips for fresh graduates – Career Newsletter”.
